# Museum asset pack credit

`MuseumAsset.fbx` and `ColorPaletteMuseum.png` are the free tier of the **Museum Asset Pack** by Voloshka (viravoloshyn.itch.io/museum-asset-pack) — a modular 3D museum kit (columns, pedestals, sculptures, rope barriers, display cases, benches, wall/floor/arch modules).

The pack's page states it's "completely free for any kind of use" and "feel free to use both in any type of project." No formal license document is provided beyond that statement.

Only a curated subset of the ~95 pieces is used here (see `components/museum/MuseumAssetPieces.tsx`), not the full assembled kit.
